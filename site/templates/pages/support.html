Getting started

  1. 1

    Install

    Open the download, drag Shelf to Applications and launch it. The Shelf icon appears in your menu bar.

  2. 2

    Make a shelf

    Press ⌥⌘N or drag a file onto the menu bar icon. Name it, pick a color, and drop in whatever belongs there.

  3. 3

    Add a Space

    Open the menu, choose New Space, and move shelves into it. Switch between Spaces with ⌘⁠1⁠–⁠9.

  4. 4

    Teach it a rule

    In Settings → Rules, pick a folder and a condition. Preview what it catches, then switch it on.

Troubleshooting

Common questions.

Shelf's global shortcut doesn't work.

Shelf needs Accessibility permission for global shortcuts. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and make sure Shelf is on. If it already is, toggle it off and on again.

A shelf disappeared after I unplugged my display.

Shelves wait for their display to come back. To bring one to your current screen, open the menu, hold ⌥ and choose Gather shelves here.

How do I back up my layout?

Choose File → Export Layout in Settings. You get a small .shelfspace file you can keep anywhere or import on another Mac.

Can Shelf sync between my Macs?

Not automatically. Shelf keeps everything local on purpose. Exporting and importing a layout takes a few seconds.

How do I uninstall Shelf?

Quit Shelf from its menu, then drag it from Applications to the Trash. Your files are never touched, since shelves only hold references.

Lost your license key?

Enter your purchase email in Settings → License → Recover and we'll resend it right away.
