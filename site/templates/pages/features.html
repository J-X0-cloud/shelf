At a glance

Nine things Shelf does well.

{{icon shelves}}

Floating shelves

Resizable panels for files, folders, apps and links. Snap them to edges, stack them, or collapse them to a tab.

{{icon spaces}}

Spaces

Saved sets of shelves for each project. Switch from the menu bar or with ⌘⁠1⁠–⁠9.

{{icon wand}}

Rules

Watch folders and file new items onto the right shelf by type, name, source or age.

{{icon stash}}

Stash

Sweep a selection off the desktop onto a shelf with ⇧⌘S. Originals stay put.

{{icon search}}

Instant search

⌘K searches every shelf in every Space, including file contents Spotlight has indexed.

{{icon eye}}

Quick Look

Hover to preview, press Space for full Quick Look, drag out to attach or share.

{{icon dock}}

Dock stack

An optional Dock stack that always shows the current Space's shelves.

{{icon display}}

Multi-display

Shelves remember their display and return to it after sleep or reconnecting.

{{icon lock}}

Local only

No account and no analytics. Your layout is a plain file you can back up.

Spaces in practice

Monday is client work. Friday is invoices.

Build a Space for each part of your week. When you switch, shelves from other Spaces fade out, the Dock stack updates, and the menu bar icon takes on the Space's color so you always know where you are.

  • {{icon check 18 2.2}}Up to 9 Spaces with keyboard shortcuts, unlimited from the menu
  • {{icon check 18 2.2}}Optional: switch Space automatically when a Focus mode turns on
  • {{icon check 18 2.2}}Export and import layouts to set up a new Mac in seconds

Built for macOS

Feels like it shipped with your Mac.

Shelf uses native materials, follows your accent color and Light or Dark appearance, supports VoiceOver and full keyboard access, and respects Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency.

  • {{icon check 18 2.2}}Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel
  • {{icon check 18 2.2}}Launch at login, no helper apps or kernel extensions
  • {{icon check 18 2.2}}Signed and notarized, updates with a single click

System requirements

macOS 13 Ventura or later. Apple silicon or Intel. 25 MB of disk space.

Permissions

Accessibility (for global shortcuts) and folder access for the folders you add to Rules. Shelf explains each one before macOS asks.

Languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Korean.
